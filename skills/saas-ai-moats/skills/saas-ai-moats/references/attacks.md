# SaaS moats: the 10 AI attacks

Source: The Only SaaS Moats Left in 2026 (youtube.com/watch?v=KpILHX2D-AA, 2026-10-18). Details are as the video describes them. Numbers are Walling's, from the video (2026).

The model is basic economics. When supply jumps, prices fall and the product turns into a commodity. When demand collapses, you have no one to sell to. No single attack causes the "SaaSpocalypse". Ten of them together do ([0:05:02](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=302)).

## Contents
- Supply shock (1–6)
- Demand shock (7–10)
- Classic moats that AI killed

## Supply shock

Watch: [0:03:01](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=181)

1. **Your customer builds it.** "They'll never vibe-code my 50 features" misses the point: they only need the 3 they use. "My customers are hair stylists, they won't code" covers only this one attack ([0:02:00](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=120), [0:04:01](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=241)).
   - *Check:* which 3 features do most accounts actually use, and how hard are they alone?
2. **Competitors reach parity fast.** He counted 46 AI note takers (Otter through Granola), almost all at feature parity. He thinks he could start one today and be close in a few months ([0:04:01](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=241)).
   - *Check:* how many months would a funded copycat need?
3. **Open source moves faster.** Projects ship faster with AI, and non-technical buyers can now deploy open source by asking Claude or Claude Code to do it ([0:05:02](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=302)).
   - *Check:* is there an open-source alternative that an AI assistant could set up for your buyer?
4. **The platform ships your feature for free.** If you're an add-on to a system of record (a CRM, for example), your UI is a spec they can build in weeks, and they already have the customers ([0:06:02](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=362)).
   - *Check:* what happens if your main integration partner ships a "good enough" version?
5. **Custom software at SaaS prices.** Agencies now build in a third to a fifth of the time and sell a fully custom, supported build at about $299–999/month against your $99 one-size-fits-all plan ([0:07:03](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=423)).
   - *Check:* would your best customers pay 3–10× for a custom version?
6. **The model absorbs your product.** Ad-copy and headline generators disappeared once the models could do the job directly ([0:07:03](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=423)).
   - *Check:* can a plain chat with a frontier model do your core job?

## Demand shock

Watch: [0:08:01](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=481)

7. **Your customers go out of business.** Translation and transcription agencies: the volume of work soared, but agency revenue is heading to zero. He guesses 80–90% of those agencies will close. The activity grows while your customers' ability to pay collapses. That's involuntary churn with no recovery ([0:08:01](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=481), [0:09:04](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=544)).
   - *Check:* is AI replacing your customers' own revenue model?
8. **Seats go away.** Customer support is 80–90% automated in many places, so a team of 8 support agents becomes 1–2 people supervising AI agents. Zendesk and Help Scout are moving toward outcome-based pricing ([0:09:04](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=544), [0:10:05](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=605)).
   - *Check:* is your pricing per seat in a job that AI is shrinking?
9. **The thing you manage stops mattering.** Example: software for managing take-home coding tests. Nobody uses those now that candidates use AI, so hiring moved to pair programming ([0:10:05](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=605)).
   - *Check:* does the workflow your product manages still exist in 3 years?
10. **The human stops using your software.** Example: receptionist and call-center tools. Someone will sell the same software plus a roughly 90%-accurate autonomous agent for $300–500/month against your $50, and the customer saves the salary. He thinks a product that doesn't add an agent goes to zero within a couple of years ([0:11:06](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=666), [0:12:07](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=727)).
    - *Check:* is the user of your product a role that agents replace? Then build the agent first.

## Classic moats that AI killed

Watch: [0:12:07](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=727)

| Classic moat | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Unique features | Dead. Features get copied, and products converge on whoever has the best UX |
| Strong brand | Survives, but only with more behind it (see moat 1) |
| Owned traffic / SEO | 50/50. AI is muddying marketing |
| Clever code, complex algorithms | Dead |
| UX familiarity / retraining cost | Survives only while humans are the users. If use becomes agentic, UX doesn't matter |
| 30 integrations | Dead. The API docs are now just a spec for a coding bot |
| High switching cost, no data export | Weak. AI can pull data out of an app even without an export |
| Multi-user coordination | Survives (see moat 6) |
| System of record | Survives (see moat 2) |

Verdicts: [0:13:07](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=787) to [0:15:10](https://youtu.be/KpILHX2D-AA?t=910).
